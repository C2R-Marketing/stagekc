Our staging rules — in writing

Every image we deliver is labeled "Virtually staged." We add furniture and decor only — never walls, flooring, fixtures, appliances, or renovations, and we never remove anything that is really there. A buyer who walks through the front door should never feel misled. Those rules protect your listings and your license, and we don't break them.
